Keyboard and mouse

Ctrl+SSave changed files
Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Shift+ZUndo, redo
Click, Shift+clickSelect, add to or remove from the selection
Alt+clickCycle through overlapping widgets, hidden ones included (a click picks what shows)
Drag on empty space, Ctrl+dragMarquee select (widgets fully inside)
Drag, handlesMove, resize (snaps to other widgets; hold Alt to disable)
Arrows, Shift+arrowsMove 1 px, 10 px
Ctrl+arrowsResize 1 px (Shift: 10 px)
Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+VCopy, cut, paste (also between views)
Ctrl+D, DeleteDuplicate, delete
Ctrl+A, EscSelect all, select none
PageUp, PageDownLayer up, down
Ctrl+], Ctrl+[Forward, backward within the layer (file order)
Ctrl+FFind a text, menu item, widget, param, view, image or font anywhere in the skin; Enter opens it
Double-clickInside an embed: open that view with the widget under the pointer selected (outlined in blue on hover). A text: edit it in place. A dropdown: its menu
EnterOpen the view of the selected embed or goto button
Alt+LeftBack to the previous view
1 .. 6, +, -, F, Ctrl+wheelZoom, fit
H, BShow hidden widgets, outline all widgets
PPlay mode on and off: the widgets work as in the runtime, editing is off
Play: drag, Shift+drag, wheelMove dials and numbers, finely; scroll a scrolling embed
Play: double-clickReset a dial or number, or edit an editable one (Enter commits, Escape cancels)
Play: arrows, Enter, EscMove in an open menu, choose, close
